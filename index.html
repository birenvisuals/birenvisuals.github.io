About Biren Visuals

I am Biren Murmu, a 3D Furniture Artist building Biren Visuals as a premier digital brand for architectural furniture assets.

My philosophy centers on specialization and craftsmanship. Instead of modeling generic objects, I focus deeply on mastering furniture design—starting with our flagship Sofa Collection before expanding into a full architectural furniture library.

Every model produced by Biren Visuals is built to serve multiple functions: a high-end portfolio project, a digital commercial product, an educational content piece, and a production asset for global architects.

01. Precision Scale Modeled to millimeter accuracy based on real European furniture proportions.
