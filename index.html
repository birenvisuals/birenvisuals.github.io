About the Artist

Hi, I'm Biren.

I specialize in creating premium 3D furniture assets with clean topology, realistic materials, and production-ready models for architectural visualization and real-time rendering.

Every piece starts as a study of proportion and material before it ever reaches a render engine — the goal is always a model that reads as real, from a wide establishing shot to a macro stitch detail.

Clean Topology
